## Brand & Style

The design system projects clinical authority paired with organic tranquility. It is crafted for discerning individuals seeking personalized nutrition, metabolic health guidance, and high-end clinical dietary programs. The visual narrative balances scientific precision with holistic hospitality—transforming intimidating dietetic metrics into an inviting, therapeutic experience.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Minimalist Wellness** with **Tactile Biophilic Softness**:
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Built on creamy, breathable canvases that eliminate sterile clinical detachment.
- **Precision Typography:** Grounded, neutral typography conveys medical rigor and analytical clarity without feeling technical or rigid.
- **Sculpted Geometry:** Generous curvature softens clinical data tables, intake forms, and meal timelines into approachable, human-scaled interfaces.
- **Airy Composition:** Purposeful whitespace mimics the unhurried atmosphere of an exclusive private consultation sanctuary.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from botanical medicine and organic earth minerals, anchored by high-contrast deep forest tones for strict WCAG AAA accessibility across vital clinical information.

- **Primary (`#40634D`)**: Deep botanical sage. Used for primary actions, critical data indicators, active navigation tabs, and prominent clinical badges.
- **Secondary (`#5B8266`)**: Muted laurel sage. Serves as interactive hover states, secondary highlights, segmented progress trackers, and supportive iconography.
- **Tertiary (`#8C7A6B`)**: Soft earth brown. Applied to secondary narrative copy, dietary notes, subtle divider lines, and warm textural accents.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FDFBF7`)**: Warm alabaster cream. The dominant foundation that replaces harsh digital whites, reducing eye strain during extended consultations.
- **Surface Accent Mint (`#E8F0EA`)**: Delicate botanical wash. Used for selection pills, card backdrops, intake callouts, and subtle state fills.
- **Text & Contrast (`#1E2D24`)**: Deep charcoal forest. Replaces pure black to maintain deep, crisp contrast while retaining botanical warmth.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Reserved exclusively for elevated structural cards, active form fields, and modal containers to create clear layered depth above the cream canvas.

## Typography

The type system is powered by Inter across all tiers to enforce modern clarity, objective clinical legitimacy, and immediate legibility on nutritional tables and diagnostic dashboards.

- **Numerics & Tabular Data:** Enable `tnum` (tabular figures) for all biometric metrics, macronutrient split tallies, and lab values to ensure clean vertical alignment.
- **Display & Headlines:** Tighter letter spacing on display scales prevents loose visual rhythm, delivering an authoritative, editorial cadence.
- **Nutritional Labels:** Micro-labels utilize slight positive tracking with medium-to-semibold weights to ensure rapid readability on compact nutrition density meters.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a structured **fluid grid** calibrated to evoke breathing room and composure:
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, 3rem margins, 1.5rem gutters. Max content bounding box capped at 1360px to preserve comfortable reading lengths.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid, 2rem margins, 1.25rem gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, 1.25rem margins, 1rem gutters. Multi-column diet breakdowns collapse into unified vertical step tracks.

Spacing rhythm prioritizes generous vertical padding around sections (`space-xl` and above) to avoid the cramped density typical of clinical software, giving each patient observation room to breathe.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on **ambient tint-blended shadows** and **tonal layering** rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Base Canvas:** Neutral Warm Alabaster (`#FDFBF7`).
  - **Secondary Canvas:** Soft Mint Tint (`#E8F0EA`) for grouped modules or filter rails.
  - **Elevated Surfaces:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with ultra-fine botanical shadows.
- **Shadow Profile:** Shadows use an organic deep forest tint (`#1E2D24`) at an extremely low opacity (3% to 6%) with extended diffusion blurs (16px to 32px) and no harsh directional offsets. This produces a soft, floating lift resembling natural afternoon ambient daylight.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Subtle boundaries use `#E8F0EA` or 12% opacity `#8C7A6B`. Heavy stroke lines are strictly avoided.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an ultra-soft, welcoming organic geometry:
- **Base Components:** 1rem (`rounded-2xl`) on inputs, buttons, and segmented pickers.
- **Feature Cards & Modals:** 1.5rem to 2rem (`rounded-3xl`) on dietary consultation cards, meal schedule containers, and biometric cards.
- **Badges & Pills:** Full pill geometry (`rounded-full`) for status indicators, allergen chips, and lifestyle tags to evoke friendly, tactile pebble-like forms.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#40634D` background, `#FFFFFF` text, pill radius, padding `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Subtle lift on hover with background transition to `#5B8266`.
- **Secondary:** Subtle `#E8F0EA` surface, `#40634D` text, zero hard border, pill radius.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, `#40634D` text with understated underline or chevron indicators on interaction.

### Chips & Micronutrient Tags
- Rendered in full pill radius with `0.375rem 0.875rem` padding.
- Default state: `#E8F0EA` background with `#40634D` label text.
- Active/Selected state: Solid `#40634D` fill with white text.
- Allergen Warning chips: Soft terracotta earth tint (`#FBEFEA`) with warm brown text (`#8C7A6B`).

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, subtle 1px border (`#E8F0EA`), 1rem corner radius, text styled in `#1E2D24`. Placeholder copy in muted earth brown (`#8C7A6B`).
- **Focus State:** 1.5px border highlight in `#5B8266` accompanied by a soft 4px ambient mint glow (`rgba(91, 130, 102, 0.15)`).

### Selection Controls (Radio & Checkboxes)
- Checkboxes feature a soft 6px corner radius; radios remain circular.
- Unchecked: Pure white surface with a 1.5px `#8C7A6B` border (35% opacity).
- Checked: Solid `#40634D` fill with a crisp white icon indicator.

### Cards & Consultation Panels
- Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces situated over the `#FDFBF7` canvas.
- Curvature set to 1.5rem (`rounded-3xl`) with 1.75rem internal padding.
- Paired with delicate, diffused botanical drop shadows and optional 1px hairline border in `#E8F0EA`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Macronutrient Split Rings:** Concentric minimalist arc gauges with `#40634D` (protein), `#5B8266` (fats), and `#8C7A6B` (carbohydrates) over a `#E8F0EA` track base.
- **Biometric Timeline Nodes:** Clean vertical stepping tracks connected by soft 2px `#E8F0EA` pathways, featuring solid sage circular badges for diagnostic milestones.
- **Daily Meal Journal Card:** White card with an integrated `#E8F0EA` status rail, highlighting caloric totals, micronutrient flags, and dietitian notes.
